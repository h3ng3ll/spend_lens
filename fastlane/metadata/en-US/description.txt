Your receipts, understood.

Spend Lens turns paper receipts into a clear picture of where your money
goes. Point the camera at a receipt and the text is read on your device —
products, prices and totals are extracted for you, with no typing.

SCAN RECEIPTS IN SECONDS
• The receipt is detected and captured automatically
• Text is recognized directly on your device
• Products, quantities and prices are extracted for you
• Review and correct anything before saving

SEE WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES
• Monthly totals with a comparison against the previous month
• Spending broken down by category
• Insights on what changed and by how much
• Cash expenses tracked alongside scanned receipts

TRACK PRICES OVER TIME
• Price history for every product you buy
• See how a product's price has moved since you first tracked it
• Compare what you paid for the same product across the stores you shop

STORES BUILT FROM YOUR RECEIPTS
• Stores are created automatically as you scan
• Visits, totals and products bought at each one
• Comparisons use your own receipts, not live store prices

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
• Local-first: everything is stored on this device
• Works fully offline
• No account required to use the app
• Optional sign-in adds cloud backup and sync across your devices
• Export a backup or a spreadsheet at any time, and restore it later

ORGANIZE IT YOUR WAY
• Built-in categories plus your own custom ones
• Search receipts, products and stores
• Filter your history by receipts or cash
• Keep the original receipt photo attached to each record
• Light and dark appearance, and several app languages

Premium unlocks more receipt history, sync across your devices and
cloud storage for your receipt photos. The app is fully usable without it.
