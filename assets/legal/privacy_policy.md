# Privacy Policy

Last updated: this build.

## Your data stays on your device

This app stores your receipts, expenses, stores and categories locally, on
this device, using an on-device database. Nothing you enter is uploaded to a
server unless you explicitly sign in and enable cloud backup for that
account.

## What we collect

We do not run analytics and we do not track your usage. The app does not
collect, sell, or share your personal information with third parties.

## Camera and photos

If you use the receipt-scanning feature, the app requests camera access
solely to photograph and read your receipts. Receipt photos are stored
locally, on this device, alongside the data extracted from them.

## Optional account sign-in

Signing in with Google or Apple is optional. If you choose to sign in, your
data may be backed up to your account so it can sync across your devices.
This never happens automatically — nothing is uploaded until you sign in.

## Deleting your data

You can permanently delete all your data at any time from Settings. This
removes every record from this device; if you have previously backed up to
an account, that backup is a separate, explicit action and is not affected
by an on-device deletion.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future update, the updated text will be shown
here.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app's support contact listed
on its store page.
